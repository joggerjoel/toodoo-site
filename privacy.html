toodoo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

toodoo ("the app") is a personal productivity tool operated by Joel LaBelle. This policy explains what data the app accesses when you sign in with Google and how that data is handled.

Data the app accesses

When you sign in with your Google account, you may grant the app access to:

  • Google Drive – to find, create, and update files the app uses.
  • Google Sheets – to read and write spreadsheet data the app uses to store and display your information.

The app only requests these permissions to provide its features. It does not access your Gmail, contacts, calendar, or any other Google data.

How your data is used

  • Data from Google Drive and Google Sheets is used solely to provide the app's functionality to you.
  • Your OAuth tokens are stored locally on the device where you run the app and are never sent to any server operated by the developer.
  • The app does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • The app does not use your data for advertising or to train machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

The app does not keep copies of your Google data outside your own Google account and device. You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local token file removes all stored credentials from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
